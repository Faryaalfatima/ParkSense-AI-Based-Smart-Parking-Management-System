What just happened

Each line names the data structure that did the work.

    Request board

    Min heap. The most urgent request is always at the top, and it is the next one served.

    Key cabinet

    Hash table with 10 hooks. A plate is added up and divided by 10 to pick its hook. Two plates on one hook is a collision, kept as a chain.

    Bay directory

    Binary search tree of bay numbers. Smaller numbers go left, bigger go right, so the smallest free bay is found by walking left first.

    Site map

    Graph of the entrance and bays. Numbers on the lines are distances in metres, and the nearest free bay comes from Dijkstra's algorithm.

    This page only shows how the structures behave. The real logic is in the C++ files: BST.h, MinHeap.h, Queue.h, HashTable.h and Graph.h.
